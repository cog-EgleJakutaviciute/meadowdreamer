About this
meadow dreamer

Stylist, production assistant, florist with experience, sense of humour and open mind.
In my mother-tongue Lithuanian meadow has various meanings: “Šneki pievas” (“talking meadows”) - talking nonsense; “Seki pievą” (“following the meadow”) - paying attention to what’s happening around “; Į pievas” (“to the meadows”) - wandering around, losing direction.
My eyes are drawn to them for the beauty, vitality, movement, changes with seasons...
